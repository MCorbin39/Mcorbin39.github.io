About Indianapolis Slippi Rankings

This website is a community resource for tracking Indianapolis Super Smash Bros. Melee players by their online Slippi points.

How Rankings Work

Players are listed in descending order by their total points. Use the Rankings page to add a player, update the list by removing a player, or review the current standings.

Managing Players

Player tags must be unique. Adding a player requires a tag and a non-negative whole-number point total. Deleting a player requires confirming the matching tag before the entry is removed.
